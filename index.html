Executive Context: The Pricing Disparity Paradox

This analysis addresses the critical managerial challenge facing Louis Vuitton leadership: mitigating the >40% regional price gap between mainland China and Europe without compromising our core market base. Raising European prices to force global price parity dampens demand among local European consumers already facing economic austerity. The strategic imperatives below demonstrate why protecting local European market resilience must take priority over quick-fix arbitrage narrowing.

Executive Context: Decoupling Pricing from Tourist Arbitrage

To eliminate grey-market arbitrage while safeguarding our European customer base, Louis Vuitton must implement a 3-pillar structural response. By freezing prices in Europe, placing strict global purchase caps on high-arbitrage canvas lines, and elevating domestic Chinese VIP offerings into bespoke non-logo categories, LV achieves sustainable growth without diluting brand equity.
